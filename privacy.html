Privacy Policy for Raki Bot

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Raki Bot ("Raki", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you add or interact with Raki Bot on Discord.

2. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our services, Raki Bot collects limited data necessary for its functionality, including:

  • Discord Identifiers: Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs.
  • Message Content & Interactions: Text messages, commands, and image inputs sent in public text channels where Raki Bot has permission to read messages.
  • User Feedback: Reaction data (e.g., 👍/👎 reactions) used for bot quality assessment and RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback).

3. How We Use Your Data & Third-Party Sharing

We use the collected information to process user queries, maintain custom bot configurations, and enhance user engagement within Discord servers.

Important Notice Regarding Free Tier & AI Training: For users and servers utilizing the free tier of Raki Bot, public message interactions, feedback, and prompt data may be processed or shared with third-party Artificial Intelligence service providers (such as Google Gemini API) for the purpose of training, fine-tuning, and optimizing our conversational AI models and performance.

4. Data Retention and Storage

We retain collected data only as long as necessary to provide the bot's features and improve our services. Data is securely stored using industry-standard database systems (including Firebase).

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

Server administrators or individual users may request the deletion of their stored data or opt out at any time by contacting us through our official support server (MON Community).

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our official support server on Discord: MON Community.
